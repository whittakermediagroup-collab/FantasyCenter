Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Draft Command is a fantasy football draft assistant. This page explains, plainly, what happens to your data when you use it.

The short version

We don't collect any of your data. There's no login, no account, no server that receives or stores anything about you. Your draft picks are saved only in your own browser, on your own device, and are never sent anywhere.

What's stored on your device

When you draft a player, undo a pick, or set your draft slot, that information is saved using your browser's local storage (localStorage) so your progress survives a page reload. This data stays entirely on your device — it is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, and we have no way to see it. Clearing your browser's site data, or using a different browser or device, will not carry it over, since it was never anywhere but your own browser.

No third-party trackers, ads, or analytics

This site loads no fonts, scripts, or resources from any third-party service. Everything it needs — the app itself, its styling, and its fonts — is bundled and served directly from this site. There are no ads, no analytics scripts, and no tracking of any kind.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives standard technical information from your browser as part of normal web traffic (such as your IP address and browser type) in order to deliver the page to you. This is standard for any website and is governed by GitHub's own Privacy Statement, not by us.

Where the player data comes from

The player, injury, ADP, and usage data shown in the app is pulled automatically from public sports data sources (including ESPN and nflverse) on a daily schedule, to keep it current. This happens on our own infrastructure, independent of any visitor using the site — it has nothing to do with you or your browsing activity.

Changes to this policy

If what this site collects or how it works ever changes, this page will be updated to reflect that honestly.
